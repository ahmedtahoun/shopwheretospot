About us

Where To Spot

Marketing, creative production and business products — based in Giza, Egypt.

Where To Spot helps businesses get found, get reviewed and win customers. We started as a local discovery platform connecting people with places and services, and grew into a full-service marketing and creative studio. Today we bring lead generation, print, fabrication, video, digital and AI under one roof, so a brand looks and performs the same everywhere it appears.

Lead-generation plansMonthly marketing plans from EGP 299 to EGP 1,499: ad campaigns, landing pages, funnels, CRM and automation.
NFC review productsTap-to-review Google cards, keychains and countertop NFC + QR stands from EGP 200.
SkincareAuthentic Korean skincare from The Face Shop and Round Lab.
Custom productionPrinting, signage, acrylic displays, event booths, video, websites and AI tools — quoted per project in EGP.

Who we work with

Gyms, restaurants and cafés, clinics, real-estate companies, beauty brands, retailers and car dealers across Egypt and the region.
