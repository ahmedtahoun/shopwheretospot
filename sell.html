Questions

Do I pay anything to join?

No. There’s no fee to list your products. We only take the agreed commission on orders that are delivered.

Who sets the price?

You tell us your price, and we agree the shop price and the commission together before anything goes live.

What can I sell?

Coffee, skincare, food and sweets, handmade gifts, fashion, home decor, pet products and business products. If it’s good and you can supply it, tell us.
