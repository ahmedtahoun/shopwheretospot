Delivery & shipping policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Where we deliver

We deliver to all 27 governorates in Egypt. We do not currently ship outside Egypt.

2. Delivery times

AreaEstimated delivery
Cairo & Giza1–3 business days
Alexandria, Delta and Canal cities2–5 business days
Upper Egypt, Red Sea, Sinai, Matrouh and New Valley3–7 business days

Orders are processed within 1 business day (Sunday–Thursday, excluding public holidays). Custom-made items are delivered on the date agreed in your quotation.

3. Delivery fees

Order value (products)Delivery fee
Under EGP 1,500EGP 75 flat rate, anywhere in Egypt
EGP 1,500 or moreFree

The delivery fee is shown in your cart before you place your order. Marketing plans and digital services are delivered online and have no delivery fee.

4. Payment on delivery

You can pay cash on delivery. Please have the exact amount in EGP ready. Card payment will be available at checkout once enabled.

5. Order confirmation and tracking

We confirm every order by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch. You can check your order status with “Track your order” on the shop page, or by contacting us with your order number.

6. Failed deliveries

The courier will try to reach you up to two times. If delivery fails because we can’t reach you or the address is incorrect, we will contact you to arrange redelivery, which may carry an extra delivery fee.

7. Damaged parcels

Please check your parcel when it arrives. If it is damaged, contact us within 48 hours with photos and we will replace the item or refund you — see our Refund & cancellation policy.
