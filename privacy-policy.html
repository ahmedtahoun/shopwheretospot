Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how Where To Spot (“we”, “us”), located at MQR, El Wahat Road, 6th of October City, Giza, Egypt, collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit shop.wheretospot.com or buy from us. We process personal data in line with Egypt’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020.

1. Data we collect

  • Order details: name, email, phone number, delivery address, governorate and the items you order.
  • Enquiries: the details you send through our forms, the chat assistant on our website (what you need, your name and your phone number or email), email, phone or WhatsApp. We use them only to reply to you and prepare your quote.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information, and data stored in your browser (such as your cart and wishlist) so the site works.
  • Payment data: if you pay by card or bank transfer, payment is handled by our licensed payment provider. We do not see or store your full card number, CVV or banking passwords.

2. How we use your data

  • To process, deliver and support your orders and subscriptions.
  • To answer your questions and provide customer service.
  • To send order updates, receipts and — only if you agree — offers. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

3. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share only what is needed with:

  • Delivery companies, to deliver your order.
  • Payment providers, to process payments securely.
  • Service providers who host our website or help us run our business, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, when required by Egyptian law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as required by Egyptian tax and commercial law, and other data only as long as needed for the purposes above.

5. Security

Our site uses HTTPS encryption. Access to customer data is limited to staff who need it to serve you.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent to marketing, by emailing info@wheretospot.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies and browser storage

We use browser storage to remember your cart, wishlist and saved checkout details. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page with its “last updated” date.
